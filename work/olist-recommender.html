02CASE STUDY
01

Approach

Represent product content with TF-IDF, retrieve nearest items in vector space, and rank recommendations by semantic/content similarity.

02

Why this matters

Recommendation quality is measured as a ranking problem, not just by showing a few plausible neighbors.

03

Evaluation

Precision@K, Recall@K and NDCG@K provide complementary views of relevance, coverage and rank position.

04

Next step

A production version would combine content signals with behavioral interactions, cold-start handling and online experiment metrics.
