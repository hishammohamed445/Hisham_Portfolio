01

What it measures

Canopy cover, NDVI distribution, vegetation fractions, image-quality indicators, vapor-pressure deficit and canopy-air temperature delta.

02

Engineering choices

Inputs are validated aggressively; RGB and NIR dimensions must match; quality warnings remain visible instead of silently discarding data.

03

Reproducibility

Synthetic fixtures make the repository testable without bundling a large or ambiguously licensed dataset.

04

Boundary

The current stress score is explicitly labelled heuristic. Production use still requires calibrated geospatial inputs, stronger registration and ground-truth validation.
