02CASE STUDY
01

The goal

Connect every truck visit to verified visual evidence, count pallet movements without double-counting, and carry those events into warehouse and ERP workflows.

02

Architecture

Cameras and readers feed edge inference and tracking workers; an event engine writes durable events, evidence and review state into the application zone and data layer.

03

Current evidence

Public logistics data has been cleaned and validated for a detection baseline. Factory-specific movement, identity and acceptance data is still awaiting site collection.

04

Integrity gate

No trained model is represented as complete yet. Plate data with unresolved usage terms is isolated from training until permission is documented.
