02CASE STUDY
01

The problem

Industrial cameras record enormous amounts of footage, but safety teams still need timely, reviewable events instead of raw video.

02

What I built

I worked across product direction, backend platform, pilot metrics, customer discovery and partnerships while co-building an edge-first perception pipeline that turns detections into evidence-backed incidents.

03

System thinking

The runtime combines object detection, tracking, temporal rules, zone logic and event aggregation, then pushes incidents through FastAPI, PostgreSQL, Redis and WebSockets to operational interfaces.

04

Design principle

Human-in-the-loop by default: the system supports safety teams with evidence and workflow, while final decisions remain with authorized HSE professionals.
